Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know.

Have a question not answered here? Contact Stacey directly.

What format are the documents delivered in?
All 58+ documents are delivered as Microsoft Word (.docx) files, organized in a ZIP package. Every document uses yellow fill-in fields to guide customization — simply open in Word and fill in your facility's specific information.
Does this package work for my accreditation body?
Yes. The package is built to meet the standards of all four major ASC accreditation bodies — AAAHC, AAAASF, The Joint Commission, and DNV Healthcare — as well as CMS Conditions for Coverage and applicable state regulations. Fill-in fields guide you to specify your chosen accreditation body throughout.
Can I use this for a new ASC that isn't open yet?
Absolutely — this package was designed with new ASC startups in mind. The ASC Startup Master Checklist walks you through every step from entity formation to first patient, including licensing, credentialing, NPDB registration, accreditation application, and EMR go-live.
What if my state has specific regulations?
Every document includes yellow fill-in fields for state-specific citations, agency contacts, and regulatory references. The Complete and Concierge packages include assistance updating state-specific language for your jurisdiction.
How is this different from generic policy templates online?
This is not a template collection — it is a complete operational infrastructure system built by an experienced ASC nurse. Every document is written by someone who has worked surgical cases, stood in survey rooms, managed QAPI programs, and credentialed providers. The content reflects real clinical and operational expertise, not generic language.
Do I need to update these documents over time?
Yes — all policies should be reviewed annually and whenever regulations change. Every document is built for easy annual review with governance adoption signature lines. The QAPI Annual Work Plan in your package includes an annual policy review cycle to keep you on track.
Can I add my facility logo to all the documents?
Yes — every document includes a logo placeholder box with step-by-step instructions. The CS-00 Logo Setup Page in your package walks you through the process. Simply insert your logo file into each document using Word's Insert > Pictures function.
How quickly can I receive the package?
The Essential package is delivered immediately upon payment. Complete and Concierge packages require a brief intake to gather your facility information for customization — typically 3–5 business days for delivery after intake is complete.
